Shape Sensing of Multimode Optical Fiber via Neural Network

Visiting research · Summer 2025

Harvard Medical School · Wellman Center · Bouma Lab

Figure coming soon

Abstract

I trained a CNN-based network to predict the shape of a multimode optical fiber from sparse transmission-matrix data, achieving over 98% prediction accuracy for a 1 m fiber, and analyzed how fiber length and the number of shape degrees of freedom affect predictability.

Overview

The transmission matrix of a multimode fiber changes as the fiber bends, which makes it a potential shape sensor for flexible endoscopy. The challenge is inferring shape from only sparse measurements of that matrix.

What I did

  • Developed a CNN-based model predicting fiber shape from sparse transmission-matrix data.
  • Achieved over 98% prediction accuracy for a 1 m multimode fiber.
  • Analyzed the impact of fiber length and shape degrees of freedom on prediction performance.
