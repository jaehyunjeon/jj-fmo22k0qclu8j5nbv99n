Fourier Neural Operator Surrogate Solver for Freeform Metamaterials

Research project

KAIST · Quantum Nano Optics Lab

Figure coming soon

Abstract

I implemented a Fourier Neural Operator–based deep network that predicts the electromagnetic field distribution of freeform metamaterials across a broad wavelength range, replacing repeated full-wave simulations. Using the trained surrogate, I built an inverse-design framework for diverse device functionalities.

Overview

Full-wave simulation is the main cost of freeform inverse design. A neural-operator surrogate that maps geometry to fields can make each forward and gradient evaluation far cheaper.

What I did

  • Implemented a Fourier Neural Operator surrogate solver predicting electromagnetic field distributions of freeform metamaterials over a broad wavelength range.
  • Built an inverse-design framework on top of the surrogate for diverse functionalities.
